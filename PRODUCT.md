# Product: Hydro-Pro quote widget (pitch demo)

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS; the widget is a self-contained block (style + markup + script) that can be pasted into a GoDaddy Website Builder "HTML" section or iframed.

## Users
1. **The owner of Hydro-Pro Power Washing LLC** (Toms River, NJ), who sees this unsolicited demo first. Has a GoDaddy site (hydropronj.com) whose quote path is a generic contact form ("drop us a line").
2. **Their customers**, mostly homeowners and storefronts around Toms River, on phones, wanting a quote for a specific job.

## Purpose
Turn "drop us a line" into a 20-second quote request that lands on the owner's phone as a text with everything needed to quote: service, name, phone, address, preferred day, notes.

## Positioning
Not a new website. A drop-in upgrade to the site they already have, styled to blend in (their blue `#0d57db`, white, pill buttons, uppercase tracked labels).

## Capabilities and constraints
- No backend: the submit opens the customer's messages app with an SMS to +1 732-288-5941 prefilled. Copy-message and email fallbacks for desktops where `sms:` does nothing.
- "Text us instead" fallback button (plain `sms:` link).
- No tracking, no analytics, no external requests, no prices.
- Works opened directly from disk and embedded.
